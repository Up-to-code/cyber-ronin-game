# Ashen Veil

## Language

**Route**: A playable campaign level with traversal, enemies, objectives and an exit gate.

**Route progression**: The progress of a route attempt toward satisfying its objectives and passing through its exit gate. Retrying begins a new attempt.

**Marked enemy**: A boss or captain whose defeat is required on a boss or hunt route. Required brood children also count as marked enemies.

**Gate opening**: The visible transition from a locked exit gate to an open gate after the route requirements are satisfied.

**Gate passage**: Leaving a route through its open exit gate while eligible to exit. Swimming routes require the player to be above water even if the gate is already visibly open.

**Armory purchase**: Spending coins on permanent training or a consumable pack. A purchase is granted only when its coin deduction and skill state have been saved successfully.

**Consumed charge**: A stocked skill use already spent during gameplay. A failed save does not refund its effect or restore its charge in the current session.
