# Hundred-body monster migration

The campaign now loads 100 distinct generated body illustrations, one per introduced monster ID. Four original transparent source sheets are preserved under assets/enemies/monster-migration/source. Runtime images are 128x128 with a shared feet anchor, extracted by connected silhouette rather than equal grid crops (which cut off several large creatures). `scripts/extract_monster_bodies.py` reproduces extraction. `game/tests/monster_gallery.gd` renders the native Godot contact sheet to docs/qa/monster-gallery-native.png.

This is **100 distinct body drawings, not 100 independently drawn animation sets**. Existing cel timing and procedural motion remain shared. Several archetypes have similar themes, but none is just a tint of another runtime image.

Twenty attack geometries combine with five mechanical element rules into 100 named contracts. Geometry includes narrow thrusts, successive high/low cuts, fixed-target echoes, inward pincers, sequential ground waves, rain lanes, radial marks and spirals. Element rules change the contact footprint or timing: fire broadens lanes, frost delays and raises them then reduces horizontal velocity on contact, thunder narrows/faster tells then launches, void raises lanes and drains power, blood heals its attacker on a confirmed hit. These are shared primitives, not 100 unrelated combat engines. Every attack has a fixed visible tell; leaving the marked footprint prevents contact damage. Low ground effects can be jumped. Impact art uses pixel flame tongues, ice shards, bolt paths, rune rings, and blood streaks rather than filled debug rectangles.

Bosses at IDs 10 through100 use web, eruption, rain, chain, fan, scatter, echo, pincer, ring and spiral respectively. Half health adds a delayed targeted ground mark. Spider Samurai Matriarch emits six brood specs before defeated; children have monster ID56 and brood_child metadata prevents recursive births. Main owns gate accounting and child22HP override.

Bounds: maximum12 pending marks per enemy and64 across all contract nodes. Dead owners clear pending marks. Damage is resolved once per mark after its tell, through the player's normal take_damage API. Effects do not bypass invulnerability. The art budget is100 small runtime textures, four archival PNG sheets.

Validation: `godot --headless --path game --script res://tests/monster_migration.gd` passed all100 profiles/contracts, uniqueness, no pre-tell damage, actual scheduled contact damage, and brood-before-defeat order. Parent gate test additionally checks all children must die before exit unlocks. Native gallery validates rendered sprite extraction. Further full-campaign balancing and bespoke frame animation remain future production work; this migration does not claim those are complete.

## Artist pass: measured size, effects, sound

Profiles now preserve measured alpha bounds. Enemy fitting accounts for the profile's internal art scale AND inherited boss scale: normal target height120 world pixels, boss210, with width caps230/360 for wide silhouettes. Short-bodied creatures remain naturally shorter under the width cap. Feet remain anchored; body capsule uses90% height and a bounded central core width; healthbar sits18 world pixels above the visual body. Swimming bounds inset by actual full height/half width so bodies cannot extend onto dry shore merely because their root is underwater.

Twenty attack patterns now have separate tell glyphs (crescent, thrust arrow, cross, fan, falling lanes, pincer, ring, chain, echo, web, backshot, doublecut, vents, scatter, scope, countercurve, spiral, shield, rush and eruption). Their occupied areas still use discreet corner marks for dodge readability. Five original generated elemental bursts each have three phases, nearest-filtered from a480x288 atlas. Water retains separate bubble/ripple rendering. Boss half-health transitions emit a gold radial announcement.

Twelve original synthesized WAVs cover cast/contact for blood, fire, frost, thunder, void and water. Deterministic source generator: scripts/synth_monster_audio.py. Maximum8 simultaneous voices, per-emitter90ms throttle,1000px listener cull; sounds honor the audio enabled setting and use EnemyCombat bus (SFX fallback) where available. This is elemental audio coverage, not100 individually recorded sound sets.

Artist validation: tests/monster_artist.gd passes100 measured-size/collider/bar/audio cases including1.8 inherited boss scale. Existing100 live contact tests and aquatic behavior tests pass. tests/monster_effect_gallery.gd renders the native20-pattern contact sheet at docs/qa/monster-effects-native.png. Visual inspection confirmed complete effect extraction, distinct tell signatures and readable elemental impacts. Bespoke skeletal motion remains outside this body/VFX/audio pass.
