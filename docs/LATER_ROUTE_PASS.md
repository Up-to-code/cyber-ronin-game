# Later routes and damage readability

Castle Rooftops gains an optional elevated ledge between the last fights; enemies are placed deeper into their landing platforms. Broken Sanctuary gains an elevated route above the sentinel section and more separation between landing edges and encounters. Crimson Castle Keep gains an optional upper approach at the entrance to its final arena; early fire/wraith encounters move inward on their platforms. Main route heights, exits, damage and enemy counts remain unchanged.

Player damage previously emitted two contact effects for the same hit. It now emits one ordinary burst below25damage, or one finisher burst at25+. Both use the resolved hit direction, including fallback when the caller supplies0. Hurt frame weights are25/65/40/40ms, emphasizing the recoil silhouette rather than even playback. This is timing work on existing drawings.

No claim of a complete human campaign playthrough. Automated runtime checks cover actual colliders, grounding, loading and representative jumps.
