# Quiet monastery sound direction

The rejected mix used Mountain God's Shrine for exploration and Shamisen Samurai Rock for combat, plus synthesized movement cues. The new direction replaces the selected tracks with Moonlight Japanese Harp and Mysterious Kyoto from the already-downloaded composer pack. This is an actual track change, not only a gain adjustment. Both are prepared with two-second tail/head crossfades; playback fades in over 2.5 seconds, and combat enters/exits over 2.5/4 seconds. Contact feedback stays above the music bed.

Jump, extra jump and dash now use recorded Kenney cloth variants. Hurt, land and plunge use body/soft-impact recordings; interface clicks use a quiet short material transient. Aerial-jump pitch offset is reduced from 1.18 to 1.06. Special energy/charge and shrine bell remain their distinct synthetic effects. No clothing/body cue is treated as a confirmed-hit sound on a miss.

Research:
- [Composer pack](https://momiziba.itch.io/free-japanese-music-pack): lists these tracks and permits commercial use with the supplied MOMIZizm credit. That pack license is the basis for using the existing download; do not infer rights from unrelated tracks on the composer's separate website.
- [Audiokinetic dynamic mixing workshop](https://media.gowwise.com/EducatorPortal/2024-10-01_Workshop_DynamicMixing.pdf): use mix hierarchy to keep important action audible.
- [WAFU Long Winter](https://wafusoundworks.itch.io/wafu-vol32-japanese-snow-music-pack): researched alternate Japanese winter palette and transition approach; not downloaded, installed or purchased. The page describes a sparse instrumental direction, but its content has not been auditioned here.

Verification: import and cue routing tests establish valid streams, lengths, recorded movement palette, fade-in and mute/resume. Native audio_combat checks actual output levels and cancellation. No subjective listening approval or phone-speaker quality claim is made. The track choice is a direction experiment to evaluate during the integrated play session, not proof the soundtrack now suits the user's taste.
