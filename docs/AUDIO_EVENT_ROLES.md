# Audio event roles

Research sources: Kenney [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), and Audiokinetic's [Wwise Project Adventure](https://www.audiokinetic.com/download/documents/WwiseProjectAdventure_en.pdf). Search excerpts identified the libraries and context-sensitive combat audio guidance; direct Kenney pack pages were restricted and the Audiokinetic PDF returned 403 in this session. No claim of reading their full contents. Existing local license files cover reused foley; no new downloads or purchases.

Motion and contact remain distinct. Kick motion alternates cloth recordings; confirmed kick contact alternates punch recordings with a quieter soft-body layer. Enemy sword release has its own cloth recording, alternating variant and lower pitch; enemy magic release uses a separately pitched cue. Push is a low soft-body contact cue available to confirmed wall/body recoil. It must not be fired from a button press or every collision frame. UI uses its own Interface bus so clicks remain independently mixable from combat. The existing wood click remains pending subjective selection; more bass or arbitrary synthesis would not establish improvement.

Confirmed-contact music ducking, bounded voice count and contact-voice priority remain intact. Misses do not trigger contact layers or duck music. Tests `audio_roles.gd` and `audio_combat.gd` validate imported cue identity, event category, variation and bus mapping. Subjective listening and phone-speaker quality are not verified by these checks.
